A transparent, auditable PAYE calculator for Jamaican salaried employees. Tax policy lives in static JSON files — anyone can read the source and verify the math against Ministry of Finance regulations. Generates fillable SO1 monthly and SO2 annual return PDFs, solving the last-mile compliance problem for small Jamaican businesses.
